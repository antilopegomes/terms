Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FalaDeleBB collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our web tool for short-form video clip management and social account connection workflows.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the service, we may collect or store:

  • Account information you enter, such as account name, platform, status, and timezone.
  • Basic TikTok account information received through TikTok Login Kit, such as account identifiers and basic profile information allowed by the user.info.basic scope.
  • OAuth authorization data, such as access tokens, refresh tokens, scopes, and expiration times, when needed to keep an account connected.
  • Video links, video metadata, generated clip candidates, titles, descriptions, tags, captions, schedules, and review status.
  • Technical data needed to operate the app, such as error messages, processing status, local database records, and server logs.

2. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Connect your TikTok account through TikTok's official OAuth flow.
  • Show which account is connected and whether authorization is active.
  • Manage video clip workflows, metadata, review status, schedules, and exports.
  • Maintain security, troubleshoot errors, and improve app reliability.
  • Comply with legal obligations and third-party platform requirements.

3. TikTok data

FalaDeleBB uses TikTok Login Kit to request the user.info.basic scope. This is used to identify the connected TikTok account and display connection status inside the app.

We do not ask for your TikTok password. You can revoke access through TikTok account settings or disconnect the account inside the app.

4. Content and publishing data

If you add video links, metadata, captions, tags, descriptions, schedules, or files to the service, that information is used to provide the content management workflow requested by you. You remain responsible for the content you choose to process or publish.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information only when needed to operate the service, comply with law, protect rights and security, or interact with third-party services that you choose to connect, such as TikTok or YouTube.

6. Storage and security

The service may store data in a local or hosted database controlled by the app operator. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, but no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Retention

We keep information only as long as needed to provide the service, maintain records, resolve issues, comply with legal obligations, or until you delete or disconnect the relevant account where deletion is supported.

8. Your choices

You may:

  • Disconnect your TikTok account from the app.
  • Revoke app access from your TikTok account settings.
  • Request access, correction, or deletion of information where applicable.
  • Stop using the service at any time.

9. Children's privacy

The service is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law or third-party platform rules. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. International transfers

If the service is hosted or accessed across countries, information may be processed in locations outside your country. By using the service, you understand that information may be transferred and processed where the service and its providers operate.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the service changes or when legal, technical, or platform requirements change. The updated version will be posted with a new effective date.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at contact@faladelebb.com.
